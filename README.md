# 🌾 Indian Agricultural Market Intelligence Dashboard

## 📊 Overview
This project analyzes Mandi-level agricultural price data across India to uncover **price inefficiencies, seasonal trends, and market volatility**.  
The goal is to transform raw data into **actionable insights** for farmers, traders, and policymakers.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze price trends across major crops  
- Identify seasonal price patterns (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid)  
- Measure price volatility and risk using statistical methods  
- Detect arbitrage opportunities across markets  
- Build an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **SQL (BigQuery)** – Data analysis & transformations  
- **Power BI** – Dashboard & KPI visualization  
- **Tableau** – Geographic price mapping  
- **Google Sheets** – Data preparation & merging  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- ✅ Over **119,000 arbitrage opportunities** (>20% price spread) identified  
- ✅ Volatility Index ≈ **0.6** indicating high market instability  
- ✅ Strong **seasonal price patterns** across crops  
- ✅ Significant **price variation across Mandis**
